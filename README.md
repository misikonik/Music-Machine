# Music Machine

**Music Machine** is a Minecraft mod that gives you complete control over the game's music system! Ever wanted to stop hearing the same track over and over, or disable a specific song without turning off the music volume entirely? Now you can!

## Features

- **Toggle Individual Tracks**: Easily enable or disable any specific music track in the game (including main menu music, creative music, and biome-specific tracks).
- **Weight Adjustments**: Change the playback weight of any track to make it play more frequently or less frequently compared to other tracks.
- **Global Music Settings**: Adjust the overall frequency of background music. Want music to play constantly? Or maybe just occasionally? The choice is yours.
- **In-Game Configuration Menu**: Press `M` (configurable in controls) to open an easy-to-use graphical interface where you can toggle tracks, set weights, and customize your music profile.
- **Supports Custom Resource Packs**: Music Machine automatically scans and registers any custom music added by resource packs using the `sounds.json` format.

## Supported Mod Loaders

Music Machine has been meticulously ported and is available for:
* **Fabric** (Requires Fabric API)
* **Forge**
* **NeoForge**

Check the respective branches and folders for instructions on how to build the mod for your preferred loader.

## How to Build

Each mod loader has its own separate sub-project directory. Please refer to the `README.md` files inside the `fabric/`, `forge/`, and `neoForge/` directories for detailed compilation and setup instructions.
